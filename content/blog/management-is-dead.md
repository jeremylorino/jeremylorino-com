### Let’s unpack this

Rolling back the clock hundreds of years; there was no “management” role. There was family, friends, community.

Your mother would guide you through times when your feelings were too complicated to articulate.

Your best friend would pull you off the ledge. Or better yet, push you into your dreams.

Your community would support the foundation laid out by those apart of the community.

Then as society began to scale the construct of a mentor arose. You would become an apprentice to a discipline. You would have a mentor that would guide you through the maze of knowledge. This was a two way street — mentor teaches mentee; mentee teaches mentor.


Real quick; the formula for life

> Life = the result of self multiplied by people then exponentially increased by experiences plus knowledge

### Repackaged and Refactored

> **management**the process of dealing with or controlling things or people

Now this is where I get lost. How did the we go from growing and facilitating knowledge to *dealing* with it along with those involved? Even the definition is of negative connotation.

I as a parent do not manage my child. I provide him with tools and a framework to play within. These tools will be used to create great, horrible, and mediocre outcomes in his life. All equally acceptable outcomes.

Management is the result of the failure to advise and mentor. The difference between “you shall” and “you *should*”.

Grow those you interact with and they will in-turn allow you to grow. Manage those you interact with and your experiences will become negative and the people in your life equation will decline. But hey, maybe you’ll gain some knowledge; who knows.
